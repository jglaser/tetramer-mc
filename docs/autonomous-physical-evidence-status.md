The next independent normalizer experiment should target **pose coverage** in
native-complement orthant 55 and no-entry-contact orthant 63, while retaining
all existing R4 strata, including restricted-SMC orthant 58. In the protected
guide, orthant 55 carries about 20% of the native complement; its few dominant
rows dominate the second moment much more strongly than the mass. Orthant 63
has only 29–36 importance ESS in the smaller controls. Doubling cloud intensity
reduced competing-cloud variance but reduced ESS/CPU to 0.708 of its previous
value; it does not address the missing pose coverage.

A minimal practical continuation is a **separately frozen full-R4 importance
control** with added geometric components around these archived tail families,
plus an unchanged baseline arm. Preserve the existing complete native observer,
unconditional attempted-draw denominator, invalid zeros, full mixture density,

independent seeds before sampling. Targeted design may use native labels here:
this is a physical normalization control, not the later native-blind assembly
proposal. It must neither filter the physical integration target nor rename
the failed historical gate as passed.

This is preferable to another weight-only fit: [paired-cloud reallocation](paired-guide-allocation.md)
changed held-out moments by only 0–4%. The [nonlinear-kernel control](kernel-shear-results.md)
improved several aggregate moments, but competing orthant 63 was unchanged
because its dominant components lacked enough training information. New geometric
support is the immediate requirement. A small prospective pilot can decide
whether the new guide earns a full confirmation; it cannot itself decide
assembly. A direct integral restricted to a problematic stratum is another
valid diagnostic, but combining it into an exhaustive result requires a frozen
disjoint partition and explicit complement estimates. It cannot silently replace
selected estimates from the old campaign.

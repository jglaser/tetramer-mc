# Independent contact analysis for the new SMC bridge

The [running protein SMC control](hard-free-protein-smc-control.md) now has a
frozen follow-up analysis. It will start only after all four physical jobs and
all four independent full geometry/density audits finish successfully. It does
not inspect unfinished contact weights, refit the guide or change the sampling
allocation.

[`analyze_hard_free_protein_smc.py`](../tools/analyze_hard_free_protein_smc.py)
reuses the audited stage histories and the complete frozen native-entry
classifier. Contact labels are evaluated at stages 0, 16, 32, …, 128. The terminal
profile aliases stage 128 rather than repeating its classification. Identical
retained poses use the observer's deterministic cache, while their descendant
multiplicities remain in every count and estimator.

For independent population k and region A, the endpoint estimator is

\[
\widehat Q_{A,k}=\widehat Z_k\frac{1}{N}
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbf 1_A(X_{k,i}).
\]

Population means and covariance use the four complete \(\widehat Q_{A,k}\)
values. Terminal descendants are correlated and do not supply independent
replicates or a contact-mixing ESS. Completed zero-hit populations contribute
zero without a refill. Unobserved terminal contributions remain unresolved;
there is no epsilon replacement or claimed upper bound.

The three comparisons are fixed: the historical broad-mutation SMC control,
larger IID baseline, and larger conditioned IID guide. Each retains total,
native, old-R5 native intersection, remaining native, competing-contact and
unbound masses, plus all radial, angular-projection and 64 pose-sign strata.
Comparisons use linear whole-population mass differences within three combined
SE, and a separate absolute log-mass tolerance of 0.2. Every material failure is
retained. The original physical target, normalized Haar measure, reporting
regions, full native predicate, stratum definitions and disjoint stream seeds
are checked against the completed reference evidence before execution.

At intermediate stages, the analysis reports class counts, distinct surviving
initial families, and descendants whose initial ancestor belonged to another
class. These can test the proposed loss of native-remainder families. They are
properties of the guide-dependent annealing path; intermediate masses from the
old and new bridges are not estimates of a common physical distribution.

Only ancestors retained at stage zero receive initial native labels in this
analysis. All unconditional initialization attempts already have complete
independent implementation audits, but this calculation does not reclassify
every rejected or unselected initial draw. It therefore does not report a new
initial hard-only Q0 integral or substitute the initial Hg normalizer for one.

Seven synthetic tests pass, covering descendant multiplicities, the joint
normalizer-times-indicator estimator, replenishment counts, zero populations,
missing profiles, changed stage bytes, incomplete audits, all three reference
comparisons and stratum conservation. The actual preparation also passes its
frozen validator: 69 source/reference/context files are bound. No new protein
pose, Poisson cloud, physical audit or native classification was generated by
these tests or preparation.

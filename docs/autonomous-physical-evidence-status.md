## Smallest useful next physical calculation

The next independent normalizer experiment should target **pose coverage** in
native-complement orthant 55 and no-entry-contact orthant 63, while retaining
all existing R4 strata, including restricted-SMC orthant 58. In the protected
guide, orthant 55 carries about 20% of the native complement; its few dominant
rows dominate the second moment much more strongly than the mass. Orthant 63
has only 29–36 importance ESS in the smaller controls. Doubling cloud intensity
reduced competing-cloud variance but reduced ESS/CPU to 0.708 of its previous
value; it does not address the missing pose coverage.

A minimal practical continuation is a **separately frozen full-R4 importance
control** with added geometric components around these archived tail families,
plus an unchanged baseline arm. Preserve the existing complete native observer,
unconditional attempted-draw denominator, invalid zeros, full mixture density,
50% uniform defensive support, two independent clouds, and the original strata.
Use archived samples for proposal design only and freeze a fresh allocation and
independent seeds before sampling. Targeted design may use native labels here:
this is a physical normalization control, not the later native-blind assembly
proposal. It must neither filter the physical integration target nor rename
the failed historical gate as passed.

This is preferable to another weight-only fit: [paired-cloud reallocation](paired-guide-allocation.md)
changed held-out moments by only 0–4%. The [nonlinear-kernel control](kernel-shear-results.md)
improved several aggregate moments, but competing orthant 63 was unchanged
because its dominant components lacked enough training information. New geometric
support is the immediate requirement. A small prospective pilot can decide
whether the new guide earns a full confirmation; it cannot itself decide
assembly. A direct integral restricted to a problematic stratum is another
valid diagnostic, but combining it into an exhaustive result requires a frozen
disjoint partition and explicit complement estimates. It cannot silently replace
selected estimates from the old campaign.

Once regional checks and matching-target discrepancies are resolved, the existing
[full-vessel preparation](full-vessel-comparison-preparation.md) is the next
physical calculation: 16 populations, 2,621,440 attempted draws, baseline versus
normalized half-mixture, reporting native/contact/unbound and measured-pocket
complements. It is still **inert**; all 16 population directories lack production
outputs. Its dispatcher and final-stage integration remain required. A changed
regional guide needs a new authenticated preparation, not edits to frozen inputs.

No fixed-scaffold answer, favorable or unfavorable, settles finite-system native
assembly. The [432-input finite-system bank](finite-assembly-config-bank.md)
already supplies N=12/N=24, three preparations, independent streams, boundary
controls and proposal arms; these are prepared inputs, not completed production.
The [frozen cluster-size bias](frozen-assembly-bias.md) and
[instantaneous registry observer](finite-assembly-observer.md) are available.
Their later physical use still needs round trips, reweighting quality,
initialization agreement and geometry-only/native-informed comparison. Improving
mobile proposal kernels can proceed as a separately labeled algorithmic study;
short performance pilots do not open the thermodynamic production gate.

## Subsequent bounded continuation, 2026-10-01

The proposed [eight-component geometric expansion](contact-tail-expansion.md)
was frozen from training-only archived rows, independently density-checked,
validated in two analytic sphere limits and tested in a fresh **131,072-draw**
pilot. This new work is separate from the read-only historical review above.
All original physical definitions remain fixed. The two targeted tail strata
improved observed importance ESS/CPU by 3.65× and 3.75×, while aggregate conditional
free energies agree between old/new proposals at approximately −18.79 kBT.
The [completed pilot report](../runs/contact-tail-pilot-review-v2-20261001/report.md)
retains all failures: native-complement concentration checks and 14/34 material
stratum comparisons still fail. Newly better-observed competing orthant 55
needs further coverage. No automatic extension was run; the physical verdict
and full-vessel gate remain unresolved. A bounded
[follow-up guide assessment](cooperative-contact-guide.md) archived the saved
cooperative competing geometry, but new local covariance components failed to
outperform simple allocation changes and did not justify another physical job.

The subsequent [conditional-line guide](line-conditioned-gaussian-guide.md)
passed normalization, analytic sphere/depletion and independent protein-density
checks, but its [fixed passive pilot](contact-line-passive-results.md) did not
show a useful contact-generation gain: 55 of 65 attempts to use the conditional
branch fell back to the original Gaussian. All 512 new draws were proposal-only;
the 284 additional queries reused archived poses and no Poisson clouds were
generated. Its outcome does not change any physical weight estimate, convergence
gate, SMC discrepancy or finite-system conclusion. Additional direction
diagnostics are likewise proposal geometry, not equilibrium sampling.

The [two-distance translation guide](two-distance-contact-guide-design.md)
subsequently passed 17 Rust reference tests, independent Python checks and
all 15 jobs of its [fixed passive comparison](contact-distance-passive-results.md).
It retained the Gaussian angular marginal while drawing two atomic surface
distances and an azimuth. Localized azimuth removed geometric fallback, but
whole-protein overlap remained common: only 21/122 conditioned draws survived.
Across each full 512-draw arm, native proposal counts were 32 baseline versus
33 localized, at 1.77 times the proposal-only CPU. Saved competing-tail
second-moment diagnostics worsened. No physical weights were generated and no
physical campaign followed. A separate [saved-circle geometry diagnostic](contact-circle-feasibility.md)
tests whether exact hard-free angular sectors could improve this construction.
It adds neither physical evidence nor an assembly kernel.
That diagnostic has now [completed and passed its independent checks](contact-circle-feasibility-results.md):
54/118 uniform and 57/122 localized circles have positive hard-free sectors,
versus 4 and 21 valid original endpoints. Geometry took 2.29 ms per circle on
average. A conditional azimuth proposal is feasible to investigate, but its
full mixture cost, R4 retention and physical-weight efficiency remain untested.
